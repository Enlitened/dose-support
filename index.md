# Caplet support

Caplet is a pill reminder and medication tracker for iPhone. It shows what you need
to take today, reminds you when each dose is due, and keeps a record of what you
actually took — ready for the next time somebody asks.

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics.
Caplet is free for three medications; **Caplet, in full** is a single payment that
adds as many medications as you need and lets you share or print the report for
your doctor. It is never a subscription.

Caplet is also on your wrist and on your Home Screen: there is an Apple Watch app,
and widgets for the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.

**Requires iOS 26** or later, on iPhone. The watch app needs watchOS 26 on a
paired Apple Watch.

## How to

### Add a medicine

1. Open the **Medications** tab.
2. Tap **Add medication**.
3. Type its name, then say what it is for, how much you take, and when. Tap
   **Save medication**.

### Set reminders

Reminders are part of each medicine's schedule. When you add or edit a medicine,
choose the days and the times of day you take it, and Caplet reminds you at each
one. The first time you set a schedule, iPhone asks whether Caplet may send you
notifications — tap **Allow**. If you said no, open the iPhone **Settings** app,
find **Caplet**, and turn **Notifications** on.

A reminder can be answered without opening the app: press and hold the
notification to mark the dose taken, or to be reminded again in ten minutes.

### Log a dose

The **Today** tab lists every dose in the order you take it. Tap **Take** when
you have taken one, or **Skip** if you are not going to. If you took a dose
earlier and forgot to say so, tap the dose and choose the time you actually took
it. A mistake can be taken back from the same place.

### Bring your medicines in from Health

1. Open **Settings** in Caplet.
2. Tap **Import from Health**.
3. iPhone asks which medications Caplet may read. Choose the ones you want, then
   pick them from the list Caplet shows you.

Caplet brings in their names, their reminder times, and the doses recorded in
Health over the past year, so you do not have to type them again. It only ever
reads from Health, and only when you tap that button.

### Put today's doses on your Home Screen

Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap **Edit ▸ Add Widget**, find
**Caplet**, and choose a size. The same widget can go on the Lock Screen: touch and
hold the Lock Screen, tap **Customise**, then **Add widgets**.

The widget shows what is due next, and you can take a dose from it without
opening the app.

### Use Caplet on your Apple Watch

If your watch is paired with your iPhone, Caplet installs on it automatically —
check **Watch ▸ All Apps** on your iPhone if you do not see it. It lists today's
doses and lets you take or skip each one; what you tap on the watch goes straight
back to your iPhone.

### Ask Siri

Say "What do I take today?" or "What's next?", or tell Siri you have taken
something. The same three actions are in the **Shortcuts** app, so you can put
them in a shortcut or an automation of your own.

### Make the report for your doctor

1. Open **Settings** in Caplet.
2. Tap **Create a report**.

The report shows what you take, how you take it, and how the last three months
have gone, on one page — including anything you have stopped taking. You can
read it on screen any time. Printing it, saving it or sending it to someone is
part of **Caplet, in full**.

### Keep your own copy (export)

1. Open **Settings** in Caplet.
2. Tap **Export my medications and history**.

Caplet writes two spreadsheets — your medications, and every dose you have
recorded — and hands them to the iPhone share sheet, so you can save them to
Files, print them, or send them to a doctor or pharmacist. Export is free and
always will be.

### Save a backup, or move to a new iPhone

1. Open **Settings** in Caplet.
2. Tap **Save a backup**, and put the file somewhere you will find it again —
   Files, or iCloud Drive.

On the new iPhone, install Caplet, then open **Settings ▸ Restore from a backup**
and choose the file. Caplet asks whether to replace everything or to add only what
is missing. Backups are free.

## Questions people ask

### Is my data backed up?

Caplet keeps your data on your iPhone. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a
computer, your Caplet data is included in that backup, and restoring the backup
brings it back. Caplet does not back anything up on its own, and there is no
account holding a copy — so if you want one of your own, use **Save a backup**
above, or export your history. If you delete the app, its data is deleted
with it.

### Where is my data?

On your iPhone, in the app's own database. Nothing about you or your medicines is
sent to us or to anyone else. The only thing Caplet ever asks the internet for is
the price of **Caplet, in full**, from Apple's App Store. The
[privacy policy]({{ '/privacy' | relative_url }}) has the whole story.

### How do I restore my purchase?

If you bought **Caplet, in full** and it is not showing — a new iPhone, say, or
after reinstalling — open **Settings** in Caplet and tap **Restore purchase**. Make
sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought it
with. The purchase can also be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

### Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.

### Does Caplet give medical advice?

No. Caplet is a personal record of what you have been told to take and what you
took. It does not diagnose, treat or recommend. Always follow the directions from
your doctor or pharmacist.
